# GEMINI.md

## Project Overview

Create a professional academic website for Dr. Ruth Rodríguez Gallegos.

The website should not be a traditional résumé website. It should function as an Academic Digital Hub that integrates research, teaching, leadership, outreach, and collaborations.

The site must communicate credibility, experience, intellectual leadership, and accessibility.

Target audience:

* Researchers in Mathematics Education
* Engineering educators
* Graduate students
* Potential collaborators
* Academic institutions
* Professional societies
* STEM organizations
* Media and outreach audiences

The overall feeling should be:

* Elegant
* Minimalist
* Professional
* International
* Academic
* Human-centered

Avoid flashy animations, excessive colors, or overly corporate designs.
